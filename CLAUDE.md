## Commit messages

Conventional Commits: `type(scope): subject`, scope optional.

- Types: `feat`, `fix`, `hotfix` (urgent fix going straight to a release), `docs`, `style`, `refactor`, `perf`, `test`, `build`, `ci`, `chore`, `revert`.
- Scopes when they help: `android`, `widget`, `backend`, `plan`, `deploy`.
- Subject in English, imperative, lowercase start, no period, at most 72 characters. Example: `feat(widget): show plan progress in This week`.
- Body (optional) says why, wrapped at 72. No AI attribution lines.
