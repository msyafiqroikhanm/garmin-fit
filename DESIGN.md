# DESIGN.md: Kinetik, a personal training journal

Direction: **catatan latihan personal yang atletis**. Keep Kinetik's dark green-black, Barlow Condensed numbers, Plus Jakarta Sans text and personal baseline band. Change the composition, not the identity: a session, result, agenda or observation leads; supporting measurements do not all get their own card.

Code truth: `android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/ui/Theme.kt` (`K`, `T`), `android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/ui/Components.kt`, and the screen composables.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

## References used

Visually inspected through [style.ai.id](https://style.ai.id/ui-components/), not installed as Android dependencies:

- [mapcn](https://mapcn.dev/): quiet ground, a dominant route and controls anchored to a corner. Today's activity cards give each route a quiet square of its own; the activity detail retains its native OSM map and expand flow. No map effects or web library.
- [Evil Charts](https://evilcharts.com/): put the data question before the plot, keep the series readable and reveal exact values through interaction. Trends uses one selected chart with a numerical observation, compact metric selection and optional explanation, rather than four identical chart cards.

These are composition references within one Kinetik system, not extra visual themes.

## Identity

- A runner's daily journal, read in the morning and after a session. Routes, worked muscles and actual training notes supply its visual character.
- Today answers what matters at this moment: before training, the planned session; after training, the result and what comes next. With no session, readiness leads. A supporting readiness score must not compete with the activity distance or the session title.
- Identity motif: the **baseline band**. A track, a volt band for the personal normal range, and a status-colored dot for today's value (`FactorBar`). The same idea shows up as the green band in Tren charts and the optimal zone on the load gauge.
- Copy: English by default, Bahasa Indonesia when the app language is set to it (Android Settings > Apps > garmin-fit > Language, from `android/app/src/main/res/xml/locales_config.xml`; Account opens that page on Android 13+). Both casual and short, no em dashes. Garmin terms stay as they are (Body Battery, Training Readiness, VO2max, Training load).
- Every user-facing string is written at its call site as `tr("English", "Indonesia")` (`android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/Format.kt`), so widgets and plain helpers need no Context. Numbers and dates follow the app locale: "1,234" and "1.05" in English, "1.234" and "1,05" in Indonesian. Formatters are getters because a language change keeps the process alive.
- Backend text follows the app language: flags are worded in the app from their `key`, muscle groups come as keys (`CHEST`, `BACK`, ...), and the weekly summary carries an optional `en` copy next to the Indonesian one.

## Principles

1. Give each screen one lead: Today's session/result, Workout's content and muscles, Training's plan, the plan's weekly agenda, Trends' selected observation.
2. Compare measurements with their recorded baseline or a clearly named period average. Do not turn a reference band into an unqualified health claim.
3. Use neutral ink/fill/shape for primary navigation and actions; retain status and zone colors for data.
4. Repeat equivalent objects, not an entire screen recipe. Activities, agenda sessions, records and body-focus routines need consistent rows. A route, a body map and a baseline comparison need different compositions.

## Type

- **Barlow Condensed** (500 to 800, 800 italic): numbers and the one status word per screen. It is narrow enough to set an 80sp distance on a phone and has an athletic character.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** (variable, 400 to 800): every heading, label and paragraph, in sentence case. Headings get tighter tracking as they grow. Caps were dropped because a screen full of condensed caps read as shouting; the numbers keep the sporty voice. The share image uses it too, for its title and captions.
- **JetBrains Mono** 500: only for chart axis dates and small load numbers, where it reads as measurement.
- Every number uses `tnum`.

## Space and shape

- Screen padding remains 16dp sides/top and 24dp bottom. Related elements sit 6–14dp apart; `SectionHeader` adds 28dp above the heading, in addition to its parent's normal gap. A new group must read farther apart than values within one group.
- Cards retain 18dp default padding, 24dp radius and a 1dp hairline. Compact grouped recovery content uses 16dp padding and 20dp radius; agenda date chips use 12dp radius. Controls remain round where their interaction warrants it.
- Use cards to bound an action/result group, expandable summary, calendar or dense visual analysis. Use the screen background for primary headlines, baseline factors, weekly load strip, body-focus routines, predictions, records and agenda days.
- No shadows, decorative glow, shaders or glass. Hierarchy comes from content, typography, spacing, restrained fill and a hairline when a boundary is needed.
- Repetition is correct for peers: Activities rows, body-focus rows, agenda days, records and prediction distances. Do not invent different styles for each peer or replace uniform cards with a decorative bento grid.

## Components

`Panel` (hairline edge), `Label`, `CardTitle`, `ScreenTitle`, `SectionHeader`, `Value`, `Stat`, `StatusChip`, `NeutralChip`, `ChipRow`, `DeltaPill`, `ReadinessRing` (size follows its hierarchy), `FactorBar`, `SpanBar`, `SegmentBar`, `StackedBar`, `LineChart` (band, optional dashed series, soft area fill, latest point and scrubbing), `ChartDates` (the right label is the last reading's date when it is not today's), `Segmented`, `AccentButton`, `Glyph`, `BackLink`, `SyncPill`/`SyncButton` (owner-only Sync with the last sync time, on Today, Training, the plan and a session) and `StaleNote` (one muted "Offline · showing data from 07:12" line under a screen title after a failed refresh) remain the house components. `T.headline` is the session/observation style. `ActivityRow` is reused for Today's last activity.

Bottom nav: solid floating pill, surface fill, hairline, radius 28, 12dp from the screen sides. Selected icon: ink on a 52×30 track pill; selected label bold ink, others muted. Content stops above it. Labels remain visible.

Primary action: ink-filled `AccentButton` with dark text. Back links and section actions are ink text. Selection is communicated by fill, weight and semantics, not a body-status color.

Icons are bundled Material Symbols Rounded vectors (`android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_*.xml`). Structure and activity types use ink/muted; real status marks retain status colors. Account avatars are neutral track circles with ink initials. Arrows use vector icons.

Card titles carry a muted icon (`CardTitle(icon = …)`) so cards are told apart while scrolling: route, show_chart, directions_run, timer, bar_chart, trending_up, bolt, monitor_heart, flag, fitness_center, calendar_month, emoji_events, speed, map, event_available. Plan sessions use check_circle (done, volt), a half-filled ring (`ic_partial`, partial, fair), cancel (missed, low), a ring with a dash (`ic_skipped`, skipped by the owner, muted), an empty ring (`ic_radio_button_unchecked`, planned) and bedtime (rest). No sparkle or star icons.

Maps (`android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/ui/MapView.kt`, osmdroid): OpenStreetMap's standard tiles, free with no key as long as the app sends its id as User-Agent, caches tiles and shows "© OpenStreetMap contributors". A color matrix turns them dark in the Kinetik greens (grayscale, inverted, squeezed between #101511 and #969E97). CARTO's dark tiles were the first choice but now need an API key. The card map is static so the page scroll never gets caught; tapping it or the expand button opens a full-screen map with pan and pinch, closed by the back link or the close button in the same corner. Routes are pace-colored polylines; the recap map draws every route at low alpha.

`drawRoute` (`android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/ui/Route.kt`) draws a route on a native canvas with no map tiles (Today's cards and the share image use it): pace gradient low → fair → good, scaled to the run's own 10th to 90th percentile (`paceSpread`) so every run shows contrast; the legend under a route prints those two paces. White dot at start, volt dot at the finish. The same function draws the share image, in a single template color there.

`drawBody` (`android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/ui/BodyMap.kt`, polygons in `android/app/src/main/java/dev/garminfit/ui/BodyData.kt` from react-body-highlighter, MIT) shows front and back figures: untouched muscles in `track`, secondary work as volt at 28%, primary work volt from 55% to full by set count. Muscles come from Garmin's exercise catalog via the backend; sets the watch could not name show as "Lainnya" and are not colored.

## Motion

Every animation runs once, when its data first appears, and has a job: it shows where a number came from or where you went. `rememberReveal(key)` drives most of them and is keyed to the data, so a background refresh with the same numbers does not replay anything. With the phone's animator scale off (`LocalReduceMotion`) everything snaps to its end state.

- Line charts draw in left to right (700 ms).
- Bars grow with a short stagger: Today's week, Recap distance, km splits. Segment bars light tick by tick.
- The readiness ring sweeps to its score; factor-bar dots slide from the middle of the normal band to today's value.
- Big numbers roll in where they explain a change: readiness, activity and recap distance, VO2max and the plan's completed-session count. Session day numbers are context, not animated heroes.
- Loading crossfades from card-shaped shimmer placeholders to content. After 3 s the placeholder counts the wait ("Waking the server · 4s") so a cold server reads as progress. The shimmer is the only thing that loops, and only while loading. A refresh that fails keeps the cached data and adds the `StaleNote` line with when it was fetched.
- Tabs and list↔detail fade with a small slide toward where you are going.
- A finished plan session's check pops in once.

Charts are touchable. Touch or drag sideways to read a point; release clears it; a mostly vertical drag is left to the page scroll. A haptic tick marks each new point. On Trends the selected metric's value and date chip follow the touched day; its observation describes the latest reading. In "Sepanjang sesi" all charts share one index, the header shows km and time, and a km ruler sits underneath. Weekly load bars and km split rows select the same way.

Tabs reopen instantly and keep their place: each tab's saveable state (scroll, open Recap, search, picked week) lives in a `SaveableStateHolder`, and an activity, plan session or workout log opened from another tab closes back to that tab. `rememberLoad` keeps the last result per call site in memory and refreshes behind it. Loaders that pass a name (`rememberCachedLoad`) also keep it on disk, so a cold start paints yesterday's numbers right away and swaps in fresh ones; a failed refresh keeps the stale data.

## Known limits

- VO2 category scale only has the Cooper Institute rows for age 20 to 29; outside that band the scale hides.
- Widgets use the Kinetik colors but the system condensed font, since Glance cannot load bundled fonts. Labels are condensed caps like `Label`, without the tracking (Glance has no letter spacing). Each sits on a surface card with the 1dp `line` edge (`bg_widget`), like the app's cards.
- Widgets are responsive (`SizeMode.Responsive`): each adds detail as it grows, and every header shows freshness ("Sync 17:20", or "Data from <day>" when stale). They collect the cached /today (and /plan) as flows, so a widget whose Glance session is still open redraws on new data instead of keeping what it read when the session started.
  - Readiness 2×2: score, verdict word in its status color under it, tagline; taller adds HRV on the baseline band and today's plan line: the first session still to do, "Done today" with the done mark once every session is, "Finished · 1 short" with the partial mark, "Rest day" / "No session" (Indonesian "Istirahat" / "Tidak ada sesi") without a next-session caption, or "No plan yet"; hidden while the cached day is yesterday's. At enlarged font sizes the tall tier drops the tagline to leave room for the complete plan line; if its height is less than 170dp times the font scale, the plan line is omitted. Today and the 4×2 plan widget keep "Nothing planned today" and the next-session caption.
  - Today 4×2: verdict and score, HRV / RHR / Sleep / Battery, steps bar; tallest adds the first flag or the advice.
  - This week 4×2: activities, distance (load when there is none), time, plan sessions done, and daily load bars Monday to Sunday, same as the Today card.
  - 7 day trend 4×2: HRV line in its status color over the normal band, RHR over a dashed 30 day average.
  - Last activity 4×1: type icon in a track circle, date (with "+1 today" when another activity happened today) and name, then distance, time and pace (HR only without a distance); two rows show a hero (distance, or time when there is none) and a stat row.
  - Today's plan 4×2: today's session with its status mark, target and % (bar in the status color), the recovery adjustment in fair. A finished session's second line is its result ("done · 23m 24s on the watch", "short · 5.4 of 6 km"); strength shows no %. A rest day reads "Rest day" with the bedtime mark, a day with nothing dated "Nothing planned today" with an empty ring, each with "Next: Sun · Long run 17 km" (or how the block ends); no plan reads "No plan yet". A day with more sessions lists them as equal rows (mark, title, % or target), at most two, then "+1 more"; the bar follows the session in focus (first pending, else the last done); tall adds this week's strip (weekday over status mark, today on the base fill) like the plan card; bottom line "This week 2/5 done". The whole card opens the plan; the Sync control (last sync time plus refresh icon) pulls Garmin right away through `RefreshWorker.now` and reads "Syncing" until it ends. 4×1 keeps one row, the first pending session or "2 sessions done" (or "1 done · 1 short"), and the refresh icon. The plan line in Readiness and Today also opens the plan.
- When the cached /today is from an earlier day, Today's plan widget reads "Not synced today" instead of yesterday's sessions, and This week reads "Last week" with its data's age until the new week arrives. A viewer's refresh icon reloads and says so.
- Widget picker previews are screenshots of the real widgets (`android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/preview_*.png`), so they show the owner's data from the day they were taken and are English only. Retake them when a widget layout changes: on a debug build, `adb shell appwidget grantbind --package dev.garminfit`, start `.WidgetPreviewActivity`, and pull `/sdcard/Android/data/dev.garminfit/files/previews` (instructions in the activity). Take them on a day with activities this week, or This week shows zeros.
